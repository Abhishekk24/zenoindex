    How it works

    Three parts, one flow

    Contracts

    A factory clones a vault for every basket. Deposits mint shares at NAV; redemptions burn them and pay out net of the exit fee, which a FeeEscrow splits between platform and manager. A Uniswap v4 adapter routes rebalancing swaps.

    API

    Reads every basket from Arc in batched calls, prepares unsigned transactions for the app, and confirms an order only after checking that the mined transaction is the one it prepared.

    App

    Explore baskets, invest and redeem as an investor, or switch to Manager Mode to create a basket, set weights and claim fees. Signing always stays in your wallet.

At ETHGlobal we brought it to Arc: Arc builds of the contracts and their deployment, Arc support in the API and app, fixes for Arc's USDC gas and RPC limits, this site and the Android release.
